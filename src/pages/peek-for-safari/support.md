## Frequently asked questions

### How do I turn it on?

Open Peek for Safari once, then go to **Safari → Settings → Extensions**, turn on Peek for Safari,
and allow it on **All Websites**. Hold **Shift** and click any link to preview it.

### Why do some sites open in a popup window instead of the overlay?

Some sites (GitHub, many news and login-protected sites) don't allow other pages to embed them, so
Peek for Safari shows them in a peek-sized popup window instead. Close it with **⌘W**.

### How do I turn Shift+Click off for a site?

Click the Peek for Safari button in Safari's toolbar and turn off the switch for that site. This is
handy for web apps that use Shift+Click themselves, or to get Safari's **Add to Reading List** back
on that site. Turn the switch back on any time.

### A page doesn't work properly in the preview. What can I do?

Some sites detect being embedded or block cookies in embedded pages, so they may not work fully in
the overlay. Use **Open in new tab** on the side of the preview to open the page normally.

### Which versions of Safari and macOS are supported?

Safari 27 or later, on macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
