**Last updated:** 2026-10-19

Front Row is a macOS video player for HDR video and spatial audio. It plays your files on your Mac
and does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data.

## Data collection

- **None.** Front Row does not collect personal information or identifiers, and has no analytics or
  telemetry.
- **Local settings only.** Your recent files, where you left off in each one, and preferences such
  as the skip interval are saved on your Mac in the app's preferences.

## Network connections

- **Updates** — Front Row checks GitHub for new versions using
  [Sparkle](https://sparkle-project.org). This is an ordinary request to GitHub and sends no
  information about you or your files.
- **Open URL** — when you open a video from a URL, Front Row connects only to the address you
  enter.

## Converting files

MKV conversion runs entirely on your Mac using the ffmpeg you installed. Your files are never
uploaded.
